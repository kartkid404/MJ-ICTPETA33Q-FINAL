MAKATI SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL

VISION

The Makati Science High School is a special public secondary school that improves its teachers, students and stakeholders to create a conducive academic community that promotes collaborative teaching and learning through its special science and technology curriculum and global educational linkages, producing individuals whose full potentials are ably honed and completely actualized.

MISSION

We, at the Makati Science High School, commit ourselves to develop our students to become;

Holistic and lifelong learners
Critical and creative thinkers
Gender sensitive individuals
Responsible leaders and achievers
Zealous ICT advocates
Academically competitive graduates
Globally competitive citizens

Thus, make a difference in their student, personal and professional lives through imparting significant contribution to their schools, families, workplaces, local community, nation, and to the larger global villages.
